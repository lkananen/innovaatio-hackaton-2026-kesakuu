## Objective

- **Do now:** Prepare a 60-second team demo.
- **Input:** Everything from C1–C4, plus C5 if you completed it.
- **Output:** `demo_story.md` + a short recording.
- **Required to move on:** You can show the bronze→silver→gold story without fumbling.
- **Next:** Wrap-up and team demos.

## Your Tasks

1. Ask your agent to draft a **3-slide story** from your specs: **raw data → AI enrichment →
   gold insight**.
2. Write `demo_story.md`: the run order, naming the one visual that proves the AI value.
3. Record a **60-second** capture: show a messy text column, the **AI-derived column** next
   to it, then the report visual built on it.
4. State honestly whether you used **AI Functions** or the **PySpark fallback** — both are valid.

## Deliverables

- `demo_story.md` — 60-second run order.
- A ≤ 60s recording showing raw → enriched → report.

## Success Criteria

| Focus | What good looks like | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story clarity | The medallion arc is obvious in 60s | Demo story |
| AI value shown | Raw text vs. derived column is visible | Recording |
| Honesty | AI vs. fallback path stated | One line in the demo |

## Tips / Hints

<details>
<summary>The contrast is the demo</summary>

Put the **raw free-text** column and the **AI-derived** column side by side, then cut to the
report visual. That three-shot sequence tells the entire story without narration.

</details>

## Watch Out

- Don't demo the notebooks; demo the **enriched data and the report**.
- Don't exceed 60 seconds.
- Don't oversell — if you used the fallback, say so; rule-based enrichment is still a result.
